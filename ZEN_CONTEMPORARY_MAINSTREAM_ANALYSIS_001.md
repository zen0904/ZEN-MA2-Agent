## Cautious synthesis

Across the selected cases, contemporary large-stage work repeatedly treats hierarchy, camera readability, purposeful impact and section contrast as design problems. The set also contains both controlled maximal and clean minimal approaches; fixture density alone is not the lesson.

## Boundaries

The source set is not a random sample, is weighted toward large productions, and mixes vendor/portfolio and trade reporting. It cannot establish a universal K-pop grammar or replace a human visual review.
