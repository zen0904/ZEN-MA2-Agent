# ZEN Development Workflow + Roadmap 001

## Why this control layer exists

The project now discovers research directions and implementation opportunities
faster than each can be safely validated. This control layer ensures a finding
is classified and gated before it changes the mainline. GitHub/repository
history remains the durable source of truth; this work adds a concise way to
recover the active objective and deferred discoveries without replaying chat
history.

## Milestone and learning policy summary

The roadmap contains M1 Design Foundation, M2 Expressive Capability, M3
Show/Rig Adaptation, M4 Professional MA2 Implementation, M5 Focus Execute, and
M6 Production Validation. “Works but messy” fails M4; Operator Handover and
low `TIME_TO_REFINEMENT` are required at M5; no single A/B result can satisfy
M6.

Industry learning, fixture-market review (roughly every 120 days), MA/console
learning, and MA3 architecture awareness are ongoing tracks. They are not
automatic interruptions. Fixture learning teaches affordances, never automatic
fixture selection or artistic identity. Design knowledge supplies a broad
professional prior; current Show/venue facts constrain; Song/Performance
context informs intent; Zen preference personalizes among valid options.
