# ZEN Shadow Advisory Evaluation 002 — Cross-Rig Robustness

Status: `SHADOW_ONLY / LOCAL_ONLY`. No production Designer, Builder, Resolver,
MA2, Telnet, or plugin path was invoked.

## Methodology

The same `SYNTHETIC_EVALUATION_ONLY` buildup/drop song from Evaluation 001 runs
through four bounded rig contexts. Song Analysis is unchanged; only explicit
case/resource affordances differ. Every existing deterministic Designer baseline
is compared with its shadow wrapper, and every `ZEN_SHOW_PLAN` is identical.

The rig records are synthetic semantic contexts, not MA2 fixture inventories or
physical plots. They declare roles, limitations, substitutions, and any allowed
semantic relationship; no MA2 geometry is inferred.

Common energy map: `INTRO LOW -> BUILD_1 MEDIUM -> DROP_1 HIGH -> RESET LOW ->
BUILD_2 MEDIUM -> DROP_2 HIGH -> OUTRO LOW`.

## Resource-rich control — Rig A

Grounded in `TRAINING_CASE_001`: LOW keeps KEY/COLOR while reducing AERIAL and
TEXTURE and omitting IMPACT/EYE_CANDY; MEDIUM adds WASH while impact stays
reserved; HIGH adds AERIAL/TEXTURE/IMPACT but still reduces EYE_CANDY. Resource
rich does not become “activate everything.”

## Medium live — Rig B

Available roles: PRIMARY_FOCUS, COLOR_LAYER, BROAD_ENVIRONMENT, and one
COMBINED_TEXTURE_IMPACT source.

- LOW: keep focus and color; reduce environment; omit the combined source.
- MEDIUM: keep focus/color/environment; reduce the combined source; substitute
  unavailable independent aerial with `BROAD_ENVIRONMENT_DEPTH_CHANGE`.
- HIGH: keep focus/color/combined source; reduce environment; substitute
  separate texture+impact with `COMBINED_TEXTURE_IMPACT_TIMED_ACCENT`.

The tradeoff is explicit: independent aerial/texture capacity is sacrificed to
preserve focus, palette, and section identity.

## LED-only — Rig C

Available roles: COLOR_FIELD, DENSITY_LAYER, TIMING_LAYER, and explicitly
declared synthetic LEFT_RIGHT_RELATIONSHIP.

- LOW: keep COLOR_FIELD; reduce density; omit timing and left/right activity.
- MEDIUM: keep color/density; reduce timing; substitute unavailable mover aerial
  behavior with `COLOR_FIELD_DENSITY_CHANGE`.
- HIGH: keep color/density/timing/left-right; substitute unavailable beam impact
  with `LEFT_RIGHT_TIMING_CONTRAST_PLUS_DENSITY_PUNCTUATION`.

This is a redesign, not a missing-resource warning. No beam, aerial, gobo,
mover, or performer-focus role is selected for LED-only.

## Imperfect / asymmetric — Rig D

The synthetic case explicitly supplies unequal counts and center-capable focus;
it does not claim real MA2 geometry.

- LOW: keep PRIMARY_FOCUS/COLOR; reduce ASYMMETRIC_TEXTURE; omit impact.
- MEDIUM: keep focus/color/asymmetric texture; omit impact; substitute a mirrored
  pattern with `INTENTIONAL_ASYMMETRIC_TEXTURE`.
- HIGH: keep all declared roles; substitute symmetric full-rig impact with
  `CENTER_FOCUS_PLUS_ASYMMETRIC_ACCENT`.

Asymmetry is treated as an intentional directional resource, not a defect or a
reason to force a mirror pair.

## Same-song cross-rig comparison

Constant: section identity, buildup/release, two hit opportunities, reset,
repeated development, complete energy states, and bounded impact.

Changed: active roles, intentional omissions, texture/impact independence,
punctuation mechanism, and use of declared left/right or asymmetric relations.
All four choice signatures differ. The system does not append a generic
“resources are limited” sentence to the resource-rich answer.

## Complete looks and restrained/minimal re-evaluation

Medium, LED-only, and Imperfect contexts each have distinct LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH
KEEP/REDUCE/OMIT/SUBSTITUTE signatures. LOW is not a Dimmer percentage: it
preserves limited hierarchy/palette, deliberately omits accent roles, and leaves
negative space.

The restrained/minimal Evaluation 001 song was rerun on Resource-rich, Medium,
and LED-only contexts. Its low sections state both what remains and what stays
inactive; medium sections use different role signatures. This repairs the prior
role/layer omission weakness at the advisory layer, without creating cues.

## Evidence, safety, and negative tests

Every resource decision preserves section/energy reason, synthetic case scope,
and human-confirmed complete-look/hierarchy/restraint provenance. Industry
evidence remains separate and review-gated. Training Case 001 geometry remains
uninitialized; synthetic relationships are used only where supplied by the case.

Negative checks passed: no rejected interpretation was reintroduced; no
context-dependent candidate was promoted; LED-only selected no unavailable
fixture language; asymmetric selected no mirrored role; no dominant-color,
geometry, fixed-buildup, or maximum-Drop requirement appeared; and every choice
names section, energy state, action, and reason.
